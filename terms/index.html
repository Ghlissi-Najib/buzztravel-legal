WaslBuzzed — Terms and Community Rules

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who may use the app

  • You must be 13 or older. Under 13, an account is only possible with a parent or guardian's email, who must consent.
  • Accounts of people under 18 get safer settings: no public stories.

Community rules

Everything you post (stories, photos, videos, trip media, business offers, comments) must follow these rules. Content that breaks them is removed and the account can be blocked.

  • No violence: no graphic injury, gore, fights, weapons used to threaten, or cruelty to people or animals.
  • No nudity or sexual content.
  • Nothing involving the sexualisation or endangerment of children. This is reported to the authorities.
  • No hate, harassment, threats or bullying.
  • No spam, scams, misleading offers, illegal goods or services, or content you do not have the right to share.
  • Photos and videos must be real and taken by you. Fake proofs cost you the account.

How we enforce them

  • Stories, photos and business offers are reviewed before they appear.
  • Every story and offer has a Report button. Serious reports hide the content immediately until a person has looked at it.
  • You can block any user. Blocked users cannot see or contact you.
  • Photos can be scanned automatically for adult or violent content before they are stored.

Business offers

Professional accounts publish offers after approval by an administrator or the country's lead. Prices and conditions are the business's responsibility. Payments for offers and sponsored stories are made directly to the business or by bank transfer, not through the app.

Your account

You can delete your account and all its data any time in the app (My profile, Delete my account) or on the page /delete-account.
