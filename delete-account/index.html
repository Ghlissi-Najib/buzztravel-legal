Delete your WaslBuzzed account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to My Space → My profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app

Email glissinajib@gmail.com from the address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your profile, email, photos, stories, trip media, offers, scores, friends and reports you made. Nothing is kept except what the law requires (for example, a record that a serious report about content was made). Details are in the Privacy Policy.
